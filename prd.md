# Product intent

Updated 2026-11-06. The user wants to observe an autonomous world like an ant colony: people work, establish households, construct varied buildings and expand their home. The previous comparison/playback product is superseded by one continuously advancing world.

The primary experience is witnessing rather than directing. Open straight into the full-screen 3D settlement. Follow individuals, inspect needs and household relationships, watch construction from foundation to roof, and discover recorded events across generations. Observation does not stop time.

The complete local scope is needs-based residents, eight occupations including children, resource-producing workplaces, shared stocks, housing demand, labor and delivery-gated construction, migration, births, compressed maturation/ageing/death, weather-dependent harvests, day/night, varied architecture, expanding streets, daily population history, journals, and portable saves. Residents and places remain inspectable through the 3D view and accessible lists.

Success is measured by actual multi-day population/building changes, causal production and construction checks, stable repeated save continuation, meaningful visible activity, and usable narrow-screen controls. Implementation and passing evidence are recorded separately in status.md.

Boundaries: 512 living residents, 256 places, bounded recent records, and seven real days of catch-up per return. Growth responds to conditions rather than increasing forever. Families and lifespans are deliberately compressed; production is a fictional rules-based economy. A server that truly runs while closed, unlimited agents/land, AI cognition, calibrated forecasts, building interiors and first-person collision physics are outside this implementation. The user authorized a public GitHub source repository on 2026-11-06; website hosting is outside this request.

Decision: keep prior saves separate and preserve the compatible earlier artifact. Reinterpreting timeline actions as a real colony history would invent outcomes. The new game starts with 24 founders and an active construction site; existing colony saves resume their own history.
