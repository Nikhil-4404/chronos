# Product intent

Updated 2026-11-06. The user wants to observe an autonomous world like an ant colony: people work, establish households, construct varied buildings and expand their home. The previous comparison/playback product is superseded by one continuously advancing world.

The primary experience is witnessing rather than directing. Open straight into the full-screen 3D settlement. Follow individuals, inspect needs and household relationships, watch construction from foundation to roof, and discover recorded events across generations. Observation does not stop time.

The accepted expansion is a river-city simulation whose people, transport, buildings and environment affect one another. Reliable work and growth take precedence over decorative complexity: interrupted deliveries keep their progress, stalled projects recover, and construction demand includes additions and reuse as well as new land. Buildings retain identity and history while their usable space and purpose change. Porters, carts and barges move conserved cargo through loading, transport, unloading and dock queues. Residents and places remain inspectable through the 3D view and accessible lists.

The established needs, households, production, construction, migration, births, compressed ageing/death, history and portable saves remain part of the product. The new scope adds a small persisted experience-based travel decision model, stable visual resident identities, smooth time-based interpolation, worker-based simulation where supported, and visible moving Sun/Moon driven by the same fictional calendar as weather and river conditions. The learning contract is in [model.md](model.md); it is not an externally trained intelligence or a promise that every possible behavior is learned.

Success is measured by growth beyond the previously reproduced day-90 logistics stall, completed or explicitly recovered jobs, conserved cargo, occupied floor area and changed districts, additions/reuse/salvage, repeatable save continuation, visible environmental consequences and stable movement through population changes. A stable population may still produce a changing city. Implementation, benchmarks and passing evidence are recorded separately in status.md; this document defines intent rather than verification.

The expansion target is 1,024 living residents and 512 places, subject to measured runtime and save-size budgets. Canonical enforced limits are exported by the simulation; hardware support must be evidenced in status.md. Recent records and catch-up remain bounded. Growth responds to conditions rather than increasing forever. Calendar, families and lifespans are deliberately compressed, and the climate/economy are fictional approximations.

Detailed banking contagion and learned interest rates, full rigid-body cargo or river-ice/fluid physics, ice harvesting before meaningful perishability, armored coin wagons, and physically animating every accounting operation are deferred. Unlimited agents/land, AI cognition, calibrated real-world forecasts, interiors, first-person collision physics and a server running while the page is closed are outside scope. The user authorized a public GitHub source repository on 2026-11-06; website hosting is outside this request.

Decision: preserve the earlier timeline artifact and keep schema-1 timeline history separate. Valid schema-2 colony saves must migrate to the expanded colony format without losing living residents, buildings, histories or their meaning; the conversion establishes new inventories/jobs/learning state explicitly. Reinterpreting timeline actions as colony history would invent outcomes. A new world begins with founders and active work; an imported colony resumes its own history.
